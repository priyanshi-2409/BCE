Legal, Ethical, Social & Professional Considerations

This page outlines the key responsibilities involved in developing and maintaining the Bristol Community Events website. It demonstrates compliance with legal standards, ethical behaviour, social responsibility, and professional practice.

Legal Responsibilities

  • GDPR Compliance: User data (emails, passwords, bookings) must be stored securely and only used for intended purposes.
  • Data Protection: Passwords must be hashed; sensitive data must not be exposed or shared.
  • Cookie & Privacy Policies: Users must be informed about how their data is collected and processed.
  • Copyright: All images, text, and media must be used legally with permission or proper licensing.
  • Accessibility Law: The site must follow WCAG guidelines to support users with disabilities.

Ethical Responsibilities

  • Transparency: Users should clearly understand how their data is used.
  • Fair Use of Data: No selling, misusing, or sharing user information.
  • Honest Communication: Event details, pricing, and discounts must be accurate and not misleading.
  • Security: Ethical responsibility to protect users from data breaches and fraud.
  • Inclusivity: Ensuring the platform is welcoming and accessible to all users.

Social Responsibilities

  • Community Support: Promoting local events strengthens Bristol's cultural and social environment.
  • Accessibility: Providing equal access for users with visual, hearing, or mobility impairments.
  • Affordability: Transparent pricing and fair discounts help ensure events are accessible to everyone.
  • Non-Discrimination: Ensuring the platform does not exclude or disadvantage any group.

Professional Responsibilities

  • Code Quality: Writing clean, maintainable, and well-documented code.
  • Testing: Ensuring the website functions correctly and securely before deployment.
  • Security Best Practices: Using hashed passwords, secure sessions, and validated inputs.
  • Reliability: Ensuring the system is stable, available, and trustworthy for users.
  • Continuous Improvement: Updating features, fixing bugs, and improving user experience.
