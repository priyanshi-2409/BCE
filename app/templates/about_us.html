About Us

Where Bristol Comes Alive

Welcome to Bristol Community Events — your gateway to the vibrant heartbeat of our city. We believe that every street corner, every park, and every historic hall holds the potential for celebration, connection, and creativity.

Our Mission

To connect people through shared experiences, amplify local voices, and celebrate the rich cultural tapestry of Bristol. Whether you're hosting, attending, or simply exploring, we're here to make every moment count.

What We Do

  • Curate and showcase local events across Bristol — from music and art to sports and community fairs.
  • Provide tools for event discovery, including date filters, categories, and venue highlights.
  • Support organizers with visibility, branding tips, and responsive design tools.
  • Foster a sense of belonging through storytelling, imagery, and inclusive outreach.

Why It Matters

Bristol isn't just a city, it's a living story. Every event adds a new chapter. We're here to help you find your place in that story, whether you're dancing under fireworks, browsing a record fair, or organizing your own celebration.

Join Us

We're more than a website, we're a community. If you have an event to share, a story to tell, or a vision to bring to life, we'd love to hear from you.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Any personal information you share with us such as your name, email, or event details is used solely to improve your experience and connect you with the Bristol community. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. You can request data removal or updates at any time by contacting us.
